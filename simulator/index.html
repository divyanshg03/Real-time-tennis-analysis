Where each piece lives

fitHomography / CourtHomography in court_geometry.py: pixels to court meters.

suppressStatic, rejectOutliers, interpolateGaps in trackers/ball_tracker.py.

selectPlayers in trackers/player_tracker.py: players chosen by court position, so the umpire and ball kid are ignored.

detectEvents, refineKink in analytics/events.py: hits, bounces, sub-frame bounce time.

computeShots in analytics/stats.py: serves, rallies, in/out, speed from hit to bounce.

Scoring mirrors evaluation/metrics.py: event F1 with a frame tolerance and mean absolute percentage speed error.
